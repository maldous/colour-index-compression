# Uniform Silesia midpoint LZ diagnostics

After the 29-file prerequisite gate, one 16 KiB midpoint block from each of the
12 full, hash-verified Silesia inputs was tested. Every candidate received at
most 1 MiB prior decoded history. All 24 hash-chain payloads decoded exactly.
One fixed chain/minimum/lazy policy was used across inputs; no filename reaches
the encoder. This compares conditional block frames, not full compressed files.

The sum of incumbent block frames is 63,235 bytes; exact selection with the 1 MiB
hash-chain candidate gives 62,484. This sum omits the cost of supplied history
and stream headers. It must not be extrapolated to a corpus size or rank. The
trial supports continuing generic long-distance matching research, but proves
neither a full Silesia improvement nor a route to first place. The original full
CIX cohort remains active and unchanged.

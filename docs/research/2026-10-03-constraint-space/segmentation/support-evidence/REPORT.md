# Queued shared-support experiment: closed at the applicability gate

Parent427ac54aa; no production/source selector changes. Twelve independently
verified existing lane-mask archives were inspected once by a newly built native
bounded diagnostic under the existing scheduler (CPU8quota1,2GiB,swap0,64MiB).
Every physical total reconciles as17header+9perlane+opaque coder payloads.
No archive encode/decode timing cell was repeated; no new archive was created.

The model whose sharing was proposed does not exist as a repeated transmitted
object in this format. Source length, stride and transform mask are already
shared once globally. Lane positions/counts derive from these. The diagnostic
therefore reports `already-transmitted-once`, not fictitious model savings.
Compressed-metadata/BDD archives were not run: they would introduce a new
representation of opaque coder state rather than compare an existing support
model. Earlier packed-metadata negatives remain the relevant measured result.

This is an applicability result, not a demonstrated tie or measured win against
a compressed/diagram codec. Four derivable count bytes per lane could be
removed by a new format, but that is frame compaction, not structural sharing,
and has not been measured here. No COUNT/RANK/UNRANK novelty is inferred.

Close this branch. Prioritise the user-directed matched representation
attribution matrix and schema-free bounded joint-layout experiment. A future
sharing experiment requires identifying an actual repeated paid model first.

## Cost and qualification boundaries

`live-results.json` includes each archive's payload, outer metadata and framing; transform rules, dictionaries, boundaries, counts, holes and literals are charged inside that payload. Diagnostic transform costs are pre-PAQ and are not added twice. Program source and binary inventory is separate; runtime/model package closure remains incomplete.

Memory is a sampled cgroup high-water lower bound, not exact process peak RSS. Research timings are single bounded trials, with declared concurrency; they are not Prize qualification timings.

Synthetic mechanism evidence, targeted Wikipedia application evidence and representative/withheld validation are separate. No current structural configuration has cleared the full promotion funnel.

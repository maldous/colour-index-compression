# Shared Wikipedia R&D matrix

This is private Hutter research evidence, not a Hutter qualification or a published benchmark. Existing cancelled full-enwik9 jobs remain cancelled. There is no Actions, Sites or submission step in this workflow.

## Entry points

Run from the repository with its Python environment:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m benchmarks.hutter_wikipedia_matrix initialize
.venv/bin/python -m benchmarks.hutter_wikipedia_matrix register-sweeps
.venv/bin/python -m benchmarks.hutter_wikipedia_matrix report
.venv/bin/python -m benchmarks.hutter_wikipedia_matrix prepare repeated_body_families-strong original --owner /root/opportunity_trials --cpu 4
taskset -c 4,6 .venv/bin/python -m benchmarks.hutter_wikipedia_matrix run-wave <cell.json> <other-cell.json>
```

Preparation freezes ownership, input identity, source/dependency hashes, complete settings, commands, budget and unique output directory before execution. A different configuration needs a new immutable cell. Verified completed cells rehash their artifacts before reuse; negatives and unfinished claims never restart automatically. The scheduler uses the existing archive-only bounded runner, including its independent decoder, inaccessible source paths, cgroup memory/swap limits, CPU lease and shared encode/decode timeout.

Read [LIVE-RESULTS.md](LIVE-RESULTS.md) for every candidate before portfolio selection, [live-results.json](live-results.json) for exact costs/receipts and [real-opportunity-locations.json](real-opportunity-locations.json) for the real-data bridge. Commands and source snapshots remain in each trial's directory. A report refresh updates the live view, never frozen receipts.

## Immutable shared inputs

`registry/inputs` identifies exact source bytes by source SHA-256, offset, length and range SHA-256. Paths and interpretations live separately in content-addressed `classifications`. `exposures` is append-only and links each event to evidence and the directing task. Corrupt or changed records fail read-back; no record is silently repaired. `input-set-v2.json` is the current frozen materialized set; v1 and its generator are preserved as superseded before execution.

The seven opportunity classes are repeated body families; field dictionaries/compositions; infobox/table relationships; numerical conditional relationships; title/link reuse; redirects/metadata; and category/URL topology. Each input is shared by several representations and coders. No candidate gets easier generated bytes.

Strong/weak/absent cases describe the particular relationship being tested, not overall compressibility. A normal XML container and unrelated repetition can remain in an absent control. Mechanistic diagnostics, raw uniqueness, observed dictionary frequencies, recurrence gaps and literal variation must support each label. All parameters, PRNG/seed, exact size and generator hash are recorded. Unrelated generated prose is explicitly synthetic rather than a claim of natural-language representativeness.

Parameter sweeps cover actual repetition distance, dictionary cardinality and frequencies, amount/type of variation, noise and exceptions, unrelated-prose mixture, and input size. Initial screens are small; 4–16 MiB and larger contiguous trials require a separate declared budget and evidence-based promotion. One-factor sweeps establish effects before compatible interactions are combined.

`input-sweeps-v2.json` records 140 materialized one-factor variants plus seven matched baselines, with exact hashes and provenance in the same registry. These are uncompressed test inputs, not compression results. Initial materialized sizes are 32/64/128 KiB. Generator support through 16 MiB does not imply those runs or measurements exist. The distance control inserts unrelated records between relationship bursts; same-family recurrence also varies with cardinality and frequencies. Actual observed gaps and distributions are recorded, so the distance label alone is not interpreted as a measured byte distance.

The fixed development ranges remain 0, 100,000,000 and 400,000,000, each 4,194,304 bytes. These are not a probability sample. The locator records exact structural examples inside these already-exposed ranges; targeted analysis is kept separate from fixed-range results. No examples are chosen by compressed size. Numerical tokens are an opportunity marker, not proof of a predictive dependency.

600M/900M remain reserved from adaptive structural tuning. Earlier whole-corpus reads prevent describing them as never seen. They are not profiled or compressed by this matrix initialization. Successful configurations must be frozen before those real holdouts are evaluated, and their exposure events must be recorded. Older 700M/850M exposures remain historical evidence.

## Controls, costs and ranking

PAQ controls pin binary hash, v216 `-4 -simd AVX2`, source basename and empty initial predictor state; state persists within each input. Control keys additionally include full configuration, input and build/dependency hashes. Caching never implies two different configurations are comparable. Every reuse rehashes the complete archive and exact restored input. Preprocessing artifacts require exact source/configuration/dependency identities and explicit decode evidence; caller-attested verification is labelled weaker and cannot masquerade as a bounded independent decode. Prediction-trace reuse is disabled.

Rankings remain separate for synthetic mechanism evidence, targeted real-data evidence, fixed development and real holdouts. Within each stratum, only identical inputs and backend settings are ranked. Missing measurements, failed cells and unverified size losers are visible but unranked. An isolated synthetic win does not promote a candidate to full-data use. Consistency across regions, scaling and resource practicality remain separate review gates, not a fabricated weighted score.

Complete archive size includes every rule, dictionary, composition, ordering stream, exception, boundary and wrapper byte. A joint PAQ stream has no defensible post-compression allocation to individual columns: raw transform accounting is shown separately and never added twice. Program/decompressor/runtime footprint remains separate until full packaging is measured.

## Shared resource budget and waves

The entire wave has at most **four physical cores, 12,000,000,000 bytes RAM and 4,000,000,000 bytes temporary space**, with swap disabled per trial. These are aggregate reservations, not per-worker allowances. Preserve at least 10 GiB free disk. The global scheduler lock prevents simultaneous independently budgeted waves. CPU/core locks prevent overlap; available host RAM/disk are checked before dispatch.

Two legacy body/control trials were already launched on CPUs 0/2. Until they finish, new matrix waves are restricted to CPUs 4/6, two jobs, 6 GB RAM and 2 GB temporary space. The remaining legacy reservation is not reused opportunistically. All new agents use the scheduler, never direct uncoordinated PAQ launches. The current conservative two-lane setting remains safe after legacy completion.

1. Correctness and discriminating mechanism screens, including weak/absent controls.
2. Independent matched development comparisons at useful scale.
3. Only compatible combinations with explicit interaction hypotheses and ablations.
4. Frozen holdout and bounded larger contiguous validation.

Priorities update from complete verified savings and information gained per research cost. A size-rejected configuration is not a proof that its broader mechanism is exhausted. Failed, unfinished, deferred and unimplemented cells stay in the coverage matrix.

## Full-dataset selection policy: proposal only

The proposed policy detects decoder-reconstructible structure, tries eligible paid representations and retains original-byte PAQ as a fallback. Body-family rules, aligned-column schemas and any later title/numeric predictors need explicit eligibility and literal escape rules. The archive must transmit its selected mode, rules, boundaries and every reconstruction dependency. Any region partition, selection map and changed predictor reset policy are additional costs and compression experiments.

There is no measured combined selector archive yet. Regional savings may not be added together, especially with shared PAQ context. No candidate is frozen for enwik9 until the complete selective archive has passed the staged evidence gates. Full-enwik9 execution remains unauthorized.

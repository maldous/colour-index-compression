## Interpretation

Reversal and centre-out are fixed permutations, so any physical change comes solely from the downstream coder's finite-context behaviour. Bit-lane partitioning is also reversible but changes coder history and incurs separate-stream framing where used. Some lane cases beat the matching same-coder identity frame, but no lane frame beats the strongest identity selection on these samples. This screen only assesses the exact requested variants and does not establish that lane partition is new theory, beneficial to CIX, or representative of full files.

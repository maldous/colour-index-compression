Mathematical representation and generator parameters: feasibility decision
2026-10-04

Decision

There is a valid, limited opportunity worth retaining: a particular asset can
have a short exact mathematical or generated description that an existing coder
does not discover. Random-looking output does not prove that no such description
exists. The present investigation demonstrates that possibility with fully
decoded finite examples. It does not establish a useful frequency of such cases
in real compressed assets, a CIX improvement, or a general recursive compressor.

The promising objective is the shortest complete reconstruction recipe found
within a fixed, affordable search. Infinite digit production is optional; a short
formula, recurrence or generator description is what supplies a possible saving.
Roots, reciprocals and other projections are candidates only when their exact
representation and inverse cost less than the original representation.

Scope

This consolidates and tests the feasibility implications of the sibling reports
seed-parameter-theory/REPORT.txt and representation-theory/REPORT.txt. The frozen
plan preceded execution. No CIX source, third-party source, dataset, production
route, benchmark, dependency, automation or publication was changed. The RC
source/readiness gate remains in force. The previously closed numeric-composition
and predictive-mixture results remain closed.

1. A finite, exact check

evaluate.py implements five prefix-separated mathematical description families:
literal bits; reduced rational binary prefixes; powers plus signed residuals;
SHAKE256 seed/bit-offset/mask references; and directly described simple masks.
Each selected frame contains an Elias-gamma input-length field and is padded to
whole bytes. Its decoder receives only the frame and the fixed grammar, restores
leading zeros through the length, and rejects surplus or nonzero padding.

These are actual serialized experimental frames, but NOT CIX archives. They have
no public container magic, version field, integrity field or distribution-format
commitment. Fixed decoder algorithms are shared, not repeated per example.

Search is deliberately finite: powers 2 through 5, rational denominator at most
1023, four generator seeds, sixteen bit offsets and four masks. Rational choices
are pruned only when their mandatory size exceeds the literal alternative. Power
search evaluates neighboring integer powers, sufficient for minimum bit cost
under this fixed-width-base and signed-Elias-gamma-residual grammar. Larger or
recursive descriptions were not searched.

2. Complete results and limits

Every one of 65,551 selected frames reconstructed exactly: all 65,536 sixteen-bit
inputs, eleven deliberately constructed 256-bit inputs, and four deterministic
SHA-256 control values. Available family candidates for the fifteen longer cases
also reconstructed exactly; 45 malformed/truncated-frame checks were rejected.

Conditional on sixteen-bit input length, the sum of 2^(-selected suffix length)

the cost of representing the remaining inputs.

The generated dictionary has exactly 256 distinct outputs at each tested length,
from 256 descriptions. Its ideal conditional rank information is eight bits,
equal to the two seed plus four offset plus two mask bits. The family tag and
length/padding remain additional. This is an analytical shared-dictionary
control, not a second physical archive implementation. Materializing a private
dictionary would require accounting for its distribution/storage costs; the
fixed generator instead defines the support procedurally.

Direct masks expose the simple whole-input patterns without a generated stream.
They are not a complete direct residual-code control for arbitrary G XOR E. No
claim of superiority over direct structured models or established compressors
follows from this screen. Four hash controls cannot estimate rare-event rates.

3. Which opportunities survive consideration?

Candidate                       Feasibility and disposition

Short rational descriptions     Exact, finite and demonstrated. Retain as a
                                narrow description family, not a general route.
                                Continued fractions can search useful rational
                                approximants; this run enumerated a bounded set.

Power/algebraic + residual       Exact and demonstrated for near powers. Retain
                                the conditional idea. Low-degree relations or
                                compact bases might improve descriptions, but
                                would require separately declared grammars.

Generator + offset + correction  Exact and demonstrated on known generated
                                inputs, including a half-bit-flip correction.
                                Retain conditionally on a short complete recipe.
                                No prevalence or real-archive gain established.

Existing checksum as seed       Actual decoder-visible metadata exists in some
                                CIX formats. A feasible way to avoid an extra
                                seed field; no demonstrated predictive benefit.

Logs, exponentials, trig maps,  Mathematically possible on specified domains with
special functions, series       exact/certified inverse handling. No evidence
                                here to prioritize expensive digit-expansion
                                searches. Function choice alone is not a gain.

Large random/constant search     Individual luck is possible. Offset, seed and
                                model costs and exponential search requirements
                                make blind large searches low priority.

Recursive expression search     A bounded search organizer, not an independent
                                information source. Retain only with finite
                                grammar/depth/cost and full final accounting.

GPU/counter-based generation     Plausible throughput engineering for a search
                                already shown useful. It does not improve the
                                description-length accounting. No GPU kernel or
                                local GPU performance was tested in this work.

4. Why useful special cases do not establish generic recompression

For an n-bit integer X, the rational-prefix code succeeds exactly when
    q*X <= 2^n*p < q*(X+1).
This is integer arithmetic, with no floating-point ambiguity. Small p and q can
give a short code. Searching rationals up to denominator Q supplies on the order
of Q^2 possibilities; reaching typical n-bit resolution therefore generally
needs about n bits of combined numerator/denominator choice. This counting
heuristic is not a universal claim about every X or every rational code.

For X = a^k + d, short a and very small d are genuinely favorable. Generic
residuals between successive powers can require about (1-1/k)*n bits, alongside
about n/k bits for the root. The present signed gamma residual is deliberately
simple and can take roughly twice the residual's binary magnitude length.
Its control losses do not prove that all optimized power-based codes lose.
An interval/rank coder could remove waste; it cannot evade the finite-source
counting constraint merely by moving bits between a and d.

With an ideal independent uniform reference, an n-bit exact match at any one
candidate occurs with probability 2^-n. Trying M candidates gives probability
at most M*2^-n by the union bound; overlapping candidates need not be independent.
Offsets normally need about log2(M) bits when identifying one of M unconstrained
choices. Highly compressible addresses are valid exceptions and belong in the
description grammar. A lucky short-address match is therefore not forbidden.

A checksum derived from X violates a casual assumption of independence between
the seed and X; do not use the independent-reference calculation as a proof
against that case. Nor does a cryptographic hash supply an established favorable
relation. Its actual benefit must be measured against the same stored-checksum
baseline and count all derivation, function-choice and correction costs.

A fixed invertible transform preserves the joint entropy of a finite source,
although it can change marginal digit statistics and make a practical coder
perform better. Arbitrary precision permits requesting more digits; it does not
make infinitely many identifying digits free to store. A certified interval
must identify one input, or the ambiguity must be explicitly resolved. A hash
match alone is not a proof that reconstruction is unique.

5. What actual CIX code permits

Read-only inspection at source revision
b0cf82f32c20348c26cbe9dd4b107e9abcf13c4a confirms:

CIXG1/CIXG2: original-block SHA-256 is written in the 41-byte frame header before
payload (rust/cix-native/src/cli.rs:2514, 2670). The reader obtains that hash
before the payload (cli.rs:4721) and checks it after route reconstruction
(cli.rs:5084). A newly specified experimental route could receive it as a seed;
the current decode_cixg_route API does not yet receive the checksum argument.
The whole-stream checksum is terminal and unavailable to earlier streaming
decode (cli.rs:2698). Availability is not implementation of a generator route.

CIXB1: the original-input SHA-256 occupies header bytes 15..47 before the payload
(rust/cix-native/src/external.rs:1276, 1285, 1344). Current payloads belong to
existing backends; using that field for a new representation still needs a
defined route and decoder. It is not an already-supported codec feature.

CIXZ1: the per-frame original-data hash is read in FrameHeader before payload
(rust/cix-native/src/streaming_backend.rs:812). The terminal whole-stream hash
does not supply an earlier streaming seed.

CIXM6: stream CRC32 is written in the terminal footer (cli.rs:3140). It is not a
pre-payload seed in the current streaming dependency order. A seekable redesign
or duplicate field would have different compatibility/cost considerations.

These are original-data integrity values, not checksums of the compressed stream
being hypothetically recompressed. An outer recompression wrapper must preserve
decoder availability of the chosen metadata, rather than hiding it inside its
own unknown target. Count the existing integrity bytes once in both candidate
and baseline; a zero incremental seed field is not a zero-cost full archive.

Related canonical numeric code contains frame-of-reference, first/second-order
delta and patched-frame-of-reference residual mechanisms (numeric.rs:627-730).
No checksum-seeded generator, rational-prefix or perfect-power codec was found
in canonical Rust source. This is a scoped code audit, not a new route inventory.

6. Parameter and recursion rules worth retaining

Use short joint parameter descriptions when justified; independent fixed-width
fields are not compulsory. Reproducible rules derived from decoded history or
already-readable metadata need not retransmit their values. Samples of unknown
target bytes and input-specific constants still need a decodable dependency.

Canonicalize equivalent candidates where possible. Prior exact checks in the
generator report found that phases and same-family linear XOR stages can be
redundant. Different recurrences can add distinct candidates, but a larger family
must earn its selector and search costs. A transformed residual should compete
with the same direct correction language and a conventional recompression pass.

For recursive search, charge the final expression tree, constants, stage order,
length and any non-reconstructible precision/residual data. Regenerated
intermediate values are not separately transmitted. Intermediate expansion can
lead to a smaller final description, so prune only using valid bounds on final
cost. Memoization, exact equivalence checks and bounded beam/exhaustive search
can reduce cost without pretending to find a universal optimum.

Offline encoder selection may inspect the whole target: transmitting the winning
recipe makes that legitimate. This differs from the earlier HINDSIGHT ORACLE —
NON-CAUSAL DIAGNOSTIC ONLY, which selected experts after seeing each outcome
without paying a decoder-readable selection mechanism. Here all frame tags and
parameters are paid. No free per-input model choice is used in the totals.

7. Prior-art boundary

The model-plus-residual objective is established description-length/model
selection territory. This is not a claim to invent it:
Vitanyi and Li, Minimum Description Length Induction, Bayesianism, and
Kolmogorov Complexity: https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/9901014

Exact invertible numeric preprocessing is a legitimate research direction;
Taurone et al. study conditions and experiments for floating-point data:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03623
Their results concern their models/data, not replication on CIX archives here.

Rigorous arbitrary-precision intervals are an established implementation tool,
not evidence that precision creates compression. Johansson's Arb describes
ball arithmetic and special-function support:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02831

Counter-based random generation has established CPU/GPU parallel designs:
Salmon et al., Random123:
https://www.thesalmons.org/john/random123/papers/random123sc11.pdf
That work supports random-access generation, not a compression gain for this
proposal or a current local hardware performance claim.

The shortest arbitrary reconstruction program cannot generally be computed;
bounded best-in-grammar search is the defensible scope:
Vitanyi, How Incomputable Is Kolmogorov Complexity?:
https://homepages.cwi.nl/~paulv/papers/incomp.pdf

The earlier seed reports also preserve close generated-reference/offset/
correction prior descriptions. Neither novelty nor patent clearance is asserted.

8. Verification and qualifications

One run, one CPU pinned to core 8: 1.20 seconds wall, 1.20 seconds user CPU,
17,920 KiB maximum resident memory, no swap, exit 0. These are whole tiny-screen
resources, not scalable encoder/decoder throughput or per-family performance.
Setup timings are in results.json; no isolated per-family cost claims are made.
Resource ceilings were 30 seconds, 200,000,000 bytes address space and
1,000,000 bytes per output file; the complete evidence directory is also below
the declared 1,000,000-byte aggregate output budget. No heavy work was launched.

The independent read-only review found one protocol qualification: neighboring
powers guarantee the minimum code length, but not the globally lexicographically
smallest equal-length power code among ALL valid bases. The implementation breaks
ties among the evaluated neighbors. The frozen plan's broader tie wording is
therefore not fully implemented. This does not change minimum bit/byte costs or
restoration correctness. It is recorded, not silently repaired or rerun after
seeing results. See review.json for the control and symbolic-base caveats too.

This is a reference instrument, not a hardened decoder. There was no independent
second implementation, real-data qualification, strongest-compressor comparison,
transcendental digit-expansion experiment, optimal-rational proof, recursive
search or GPU evaluation. The source, plan and selected-frame digests in
results.json make the calculations reproducible. All negatives are retained.

Exactly one evidence-selected next action

Retain this as a research-only candidate for ONE predeclared small finished-
archive recompression screen after the RC source/readiness gate is accepted:
freeze a finite description/residual grammar and disjoint existing local samples,
compare complete bytes and search/decode cost against the unchanged archives,
matched direct residual models and conventional second-pass compression. This
is a recommendation, not a launched or scheduled benchmark. Require an actual
complete-byte win before considering a production route. Until that gate, the
evidence supports mathematical feasibility only, not a release requirement or
an assertion that the attainable compression limit has been reached.

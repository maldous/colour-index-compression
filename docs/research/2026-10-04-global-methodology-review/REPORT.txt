CIX global compression methodology and coverage review
4 October 2026 — literature/source inspection only

Finding

CIX covers most major lossless-compression families, but the reviewed implementation
and opportunity inventory is not exhaustive. This audit found useful additional
implementations and concrete capability gaps. None has a new measured CIX gain.
No compressor was installed, benchmark was run, corpus acquired, production code
changed or release requirement accepted.

The most useful immediate outcome is a corrected mechanism-level inventory.
New candidates should be considered on their marginal value over the complete
retained CIX portfolio, not simply because they have an unfamiliar name or a
strong published result. Preserve already demonstrated specialists first.

Scope and evidence

The audit cross-checked existing methodology, source/port, GPU, predictive,
constraint and reconstruction inventories; searched in English, German, Russian,
Chinese and Japanese; and followed leads to papers, author repositories and
released software. candidates.json contains 26 grouped entries and 71 distinct
source URLs, with current CIX coverage, upstream implementation, exactness,
maturity, license uncertainty, expected value and limits. These are grouped
opportunities, not 26 independent new algorithms.

Local-coverage-scan.json records a bounded scan of 8,832 text/source files and
matching file hashes at checkpoint 9e7e1023ee6ed621834560d9085ae08080bfe442.
Absence of a name from that scan is only absence of evidence in its stated scope.
Direct source inspection establishes the narrower JPEG bridge finding below.
Generic phrase matches in the lexical scan were rejected as false positives.
The working tree may contain continuing implementation work; this is a
point-in-time review, not a release manifest or a claim every line was audited.

This is not a patent/priority search, an exhaustive world literature review, or
proof that CIX is near a universal optimum. Native-language searches broaden
coverage, but indexing/access gaps remain. In particular, the Chinese APCF listing
could not be fully retrieved; it is an unresolved lead, not an endorsed method.
German searches did not uncover a verified German-only breakthrough; the German
research ecosystem's BtrBlocks/FSST/OptFSST and Precomp sources are mostly English.
Japanese author material and repositories establish practical grammar research;
Russian author pages establish detailed PPM and specialist-chain prior art.

Priority decisions

1. Highest near-term consideration: Pco for typed numerical streams; FSST/OptFSST
   for string fields; BtrBlocks/FastLanes/Corra as references for structured
   encodings and cross-field dependencies; original-JPEG reconstruction through
   the already-retained JPEG XL library. These are candidates, not automatic RC
   additions. The smallest useful comparison must retain original envelopes and
   compete with existing CIX specialists and whole-input alternatives.

2. Strong controls for specific hypotheses: Sprintz/FIRE before attributing a
   gain to nonlinear autoregression; one grammar compressor for genuinely
   long-range repetition; reversible zfp for scientific arrays. Existing ALP,
   FPC, ndzip, native delta/delta2 and strong generic backends remain relevant.

3. Product/workload-dependent opportunities: FastCDC/similarity grouping for
   archive/filesystem redundancy; CLP/Logzip for structural text; WavPack/FLAC for
   audio; SPRING for genomic records; ZipNN/DFloat11 for original model tensors.
   These are not evidence of improvements on the current Silesia corpus and do
   not authorize acquisition or a new benchmark programme.

4. Correct the optional-GPU opportunity list: G-ALP (2025) and SLEEK lossless
   (2026) have specific paper/implementation evidence. Existing provider
   qualification remains unresolved. Their existence does not establish fit on
   the local GTX1650/4GiB GPU, end-to-end benefit or an accepted CPU fallback.

5. Keep large-model compression as a real research frontier, not a current
   dependency. A stronger predictor is not excluded by the failed frozen-pool
   mixer experiment. No ratio claim from an external paper establishes general
   dominance or acceptable local decode time.

Concrete corrections and distinctions

* DMC is already present through retained PAQ v216; FSE through libzstd.
  The parallel source review also confirms ZPAQ SSE/ISSE. Native/exposed expert
  interfaces are different obligations from having those mechanisms in a backend.
* CIX's inspected JXL bridge uses JxlEncoderAddImageFrame for grayscale pixels.
  Upstream JxlEncoderAddJPEGFrame plus JxlEncoderStoreJPEGMetadata is a different,
  documented original-JPEG reconstruction capability. Previous gzip research
  does not evaluate it. Lepton was already mentioned, so JPEG research as a whole
  is not a wholly new discovery.
* zfp has an explicit reversible mode. Do not reject the entire codec as lossy;
  conversely, do not treat zero tolerance in a different mode as proof of exactness.
* Spatio-Temporal-Lab Elf is the erasing/recovery floating-point research line.
  ds2-lab ELF explicitly describes near-lossless model compression. Their names
  do not identify the same algorithm or exactness contract.
* Current ts_zip documentation explicitly claims deterministic inference across
  hardware/software configurations. Older same-GPU caveats should not be copied
  into its current description. Experimental format compatibility, footprint and
  actual local decoding still need qualification; NNCP/LibNC has a separate audit.
* The earlier GPU review said it had not established an ALP GPU provider. G-ALP
  supplies research implementation evidence, not proof of a production-qualified
  CIX provider or of the complete FastLanes CUDA reader.
* A numerical or semantic round trip is not a byte round trip. CSV formatting,
  JSON order/escaping, NaN payloads, tails, compressed headers and permutation
  indexes can erase apparent gains if omitted from the comparison.
* The parallel review found UIQ's historical winner combines a representation
  transform with contexts suited to its three-byte descriptors. This supports
  representation-aware prediction; it does not establish short-seed inversion.
  See the separate source-backed last-chance review.

The two speculative approaches

The other agent has now completed a theoretical review, with no experiment:
(a) shared higher-order constraints exposed by feature lifting; (b) alternative
expanded representations mapped back by one decoder, with adjustable auxiliary
coordinates. Its latest nonlinear autoregressive residual proposal is a tractable
hypothesis within (a). The relevant antecedents include enumerative coding,
algorithmic statistics, nonlinear system identification, probabilistic circuits,
don't-care filling/coset selection, homophonic and latent/bits-back coding.
The exact proposed CIX combination may merit research, but no world-first claim
or demonstrated practical gain is established.

The meaningful question is whether a short, decoder-available model explains
errors the strongest existing model still makes. Merely changing coordinates,
increasing dimensions or searching many hashes cannot create short lossless
descriptions for all arbitrary inputs. Equivalent direct predictors, paid
model/exception costs and selection bias remain necessary controls. This audit
adds Sprintz/FIRE and structured-column methods as relevant practical context,
without scheduling the proposed nonlinear screen.

Definitions and current coverage
--------------------------------
1. Literal, run-length and sparse coding
   Definition: Store bytes, runs or exception positions when that is cheapest.
   CIX: Existing native/reference mechanisms; fallback is essential.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/core.rs; cix/runs.py

2. Enumerative/combinatorial coding
   Definition: Transmit a count/model and an index among its valid objects; rank/unrank must be exact.
   CIX: Existing native rank, multinomial/count and histogram machinery. This is established enumerative coding, not a new universal generator theorem.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/rank.rs; combinatorics.rs; histogram.rs

3. Huffman, arithmetic/range and ANS/FSE
   Definition: Turn a probability/count model into a uniquely decodable bitstream.
   CIX: Native Huffman/range mechanisms; FSE through retained libzstd. A standalone native rANS expert stream is a separate unqualified route; earlier N3 disposition remains closed.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/huffman.rs; adaptive.rs; external.rs; last-chance review

4. LZ and dictionaries
   Definition: Replace recurring strings with earlier references or dictionary symbols.
   CIX: Native bounded LZ and retained DEFLATE/LZMA/Brotli/Zstd backends. Trained reusable dictionaries are a separate required RC26 item, not proved by a phrase dictionary.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/fast_lz.rs; legacy_lz.rs; requirements.json

5. LZP, ROLZ and context symbol ranking
   Definition: Predict a match or rank using context, reducing the reference/symbol description.
   CIX: libbsc supplies LZP. General ROLZ and original-order symbol-ranking coverage not established. BWT MTF ranks and combinadic ranks are different.
   Evidence: docs/research/2026-10-04-last-chance-methodology-review/REPORT.txt

6. BWT, move-to-front and sorting transforms
   Definition: Reorder contexts before coding clustered symbols/ranks.
   CIX: Native/reference BWT plus bzip2/libbsc backends; GPU acceleration unqualified.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/bwt_legacy.rs; bsc_ffi.rs

7. PPM, CTW and DMC
   Definition: Assign probabilities from context trees or dynamically adapted/cloned states.
   CIX: Native PPM; binary CTW reference-only; DMC state cloning present in retained PAQ. They are not interchangeable algorithms.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/ppm.rs; cix/ctw.py; last-chance review

8. Context mixing and calibration
   Definition: Combine predictors and refine their output probabilities.
   CIX: FixedMix and retained PAQ/ZPAQ. ZPAQ SSE/ISSE is distinct from FixedMix and PPM escape estimation. Frozen predictive trials did not qualify a new mixer.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/fixedmix.rs; expertmix.rs; zpaq_context.rs

9. Neural probability prediction
   Definition: Use a learned recurrent/transformer model to predict symbols before entropy coding.
   CIX: LSTM-bearing PAQ configurations retained; NNCP/LibNC and larger learned models are research references, not accepted native routes.
   Evidence: runtime-inventory.md; GPU methodologies; last-chance review

10. Numeric and bit-layout transforms
   Definition: Expose regularity using deltas, XOR, strides, planes, residuals and exact packing.
   CIX: Existing numeric/chain/bitplane foundations; Pco/ALP/Elf-style implementations need separate comparison.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/numeric.rs; chain.rs; bitplane.rs

11. Spatial prediction, lifting and image coding
   Definition: Represent correlated neighbours/axes with reversible transforms and coded residuals.
   CIX: Existing/reference wavelet and volume work; retained JXL/JLS/J2K specialists require full native parity. JXL bridge inspected is gray2d pixel coding.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/wavelet.rs; full_engine/volume_lifting.rs; codecs/jxl/cix_jxl_bridge.cpp

12. Structured records, graphs and executable relationships
   Definition: Separate fields and encode valid relations with exact exceptions and original envelopes.
   CIX: Retained format-aware successes and partial native ports. Recognition must use supplied bytes and validated structure, never corpus identity.
   Evidence: rust/cix-native/src/full_engine/{structured_frames,record_ordering,address_relations,graph_residuals,strided_values}.rs

13. Grammar and reusable abstractions
   Definition: Describe recurring phrases recursively and encode a grammar that generates the input.
   CIX: Phrase mechanisms exist; no complete Re-Pair/GCIS/GLZA implementation established in the inspected source. Program-library synthesis was considered as research.
   Evidence: cix/transforms.py; earlier methodology timeline

14. Segmentation, typed graphs and paid selection
   Definition: Choose complete representations for input-derived regions, paying boundaries, resets and model IDs.
   CIX: Required heterogeneous architecture and partial orchestration; complete automatic native engine still unqualified. OpenZL/CAST and segmentation research were already considered.
   Evidence: automatic-specialised-models/runtime-inventory.md; native-rc/PROGRESS.md

15. Exact recompression and encoder residuals
   Definition: Rebuild an original compressed stream from its content plus the encoder decisions needed for exact reproduction.
   CIX: Retain demonstrated PAQ DEFLATE capability; finite gzip/bzip2/ZIP prototypes retain closed dispositions. JPEG and audio reconstruction are separate capabilities.
   Evidence: native-rc/codec-reconstruction-closure-v1/REPORT.txt

16. Constraints, latent variables and model-set indexes
   Definition: Describe a tractable restricted set/model, then code the input or its residual within it.
   CIX: BDD/DAG/counting, bits-back, SDL, seeded maps, conditional modelling and synthesis have known antecedents. Most CIX variants are bounded research with scoped negative results.
   Evidence: constraint-lifting-review/REPORT.txt; rc-reconciliation/RESEARCH-CLOSURE.txt

17. Parallelism and implementation efficiency
   Definition: Reduce computation, allocation, memory movement and sequential dependence while preserving the coded contract.
   CIX: CPU optimisation and bounded concurrency remain required; GPU work is conditional. Faster coding does not itself discover more statistical redundancy.
   Evidence: automatic-specialised-models/performance-carry-forward.md; gpu-cuda/METHODOLOGIES.md

Release implications

No new candidate is mandatory merely because it appears in this review.
The existing 55-item acceptance ledger and stopped benchmark gate are unchanged.
The finite research closures are preserved; they do not imply global exhaustion.
Existing successful routes still require native parity, automatic selection,
one-executable/library integration, exact decoding, hygiene and optimized-build
qualification before the frozen release-candidate confirmation pass.

For a future selected candidate, use one predeclared bounded comparison when
authorized: strongest existing appropriate CIX control; all original bytes and
metadata counted; fresh archive-only exact decoding; total selection/encode and
decode wall/CPU, peak memory and any transfer costs. Pin dependency, format,
options and model assets; avoid corpus-specific recognition. Published speedups
on other hardware and different input/container definitions are not transferable
CIX measurements. A useful speed/size trade-off can justify consideration even
without winning absolute size; it must be named accurately.

Recommended decision now: carry the corrected coverage and ranked candidates
into the implementation agent's review, finish the existing release obligations,
and make any scope expansion explicit. There is enough evidence to say worthwhile
opportunities remain; there is no evidence to promise a new record.

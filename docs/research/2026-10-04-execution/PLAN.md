## Scheduling and proof

Root alone schedules heavy jobs and commits checkpoints. The aggregate limits
are four physical cores [8, 10, 12, 14], decimal 12 GB RAM, decimal 4 GB temporary
space and no swap. Build/test jobs use the existing resource lease and receipt
runner. Source snapshots and failed checks are retained. No third-party source
is edited. Agents have disjoint native-module/evidence ownership; the coordinator
owns the authoritative rc-reconciliation ledger and accepts individual receipts.

Existing benchmark receipts (5 verified, 1 interrupted, 30 unstarted) are
historical and never combined with a new binary as one release result. Component
tests, mock bridges and cross-compilation do not establish full engine, real
codec, or actual platform qualification. No claim of universal optimality or a
compression record follows from implementation work.

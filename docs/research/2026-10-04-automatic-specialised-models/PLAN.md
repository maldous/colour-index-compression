## Required outcome

CIX accepts arbitrary bytes and automatically considers valid file/data-type optimisations across arbitrary heterogeneous combinations of structured/unstructured, recognized/unrecognized and known/unknown data. Text–numeric–text is only an example, not a fixed sequence, a feature boundary or the set of types to implement. A finite registry of supported specialists is acceptable; unknown regions retain lossless generic/raw fallback, without claiming universal semantic recognition. Useful structure and boundaries come from supplied bytes, including late/interleaved structure, never prior Silesia knowledge. Complete mixed archives compete with whole-file and generic archives; the smallest eligible completed result wins, with deterministic ties and visible omissions. The approved scope remains one frozen release candidate and one fresh Silesia pass per FAST/DEFAULT/BEST mode before any other dataset.

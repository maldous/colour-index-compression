Final theory result

The user's remembered threshold is consistent with31-bit masks: under five
count/colour bits, k9 uses30bits, k10 uses31, k11 uses32, k12..14 use33 and
k15 uses34. A universal fixed dictionary still needs29index bits for the
300,540,195 patterns at k15. A hash collision or many-to-one projection requires
extra disambiguation; a short function can help only when the full selected
recipe plus residual is cheaper.

The fixed dictionary test used465 procedure-generated masks per middle count.
It compresses constructed masks but ties the matched short direct-function
control. None of five64KiB Silesia samples beats its strongest complete control.

The added seeded-space test directly demonstrates the conditional possibility:
a five-bit seed selects a reversible nonlinear coordinate map. Data constructed
on a hidden17-dimensional support compresses from a15,885-byte complete control
to8,813bytes (44.52%smaller), including seed, dimension, prefix/coordinates and
framing. Sharing the seed across64words beats paying it for every word.
Already-visible low-dimensional data favors the unseeded identity model.
Uniform data and all five16KiB Silesia samples show no gain. All168 retained
archives from the two screens restore exactly in fresh processes. The report
explains how arbitrary higher-dimensional lifts still require exact preimage
information, while allowing useful structured supports; no blanket impossibility
claim is made. See final-theory-screen/REPORT.txt and frozen plans/results.

Codec reconstruction validation

Four independent existing gzip documents and one unique independent bzip2 file
were evaluated under frozen paid settings. The original fixed gzip6+XZ1 recipe
loses on all four gzip files. A small explicitly paid portfolio improves three
of four, with two material gains. Existing unchanged PAQ v216 -4B identifies and
reconstructs DEFLATE on both material winners and yields17,005 versus25,138bytes,
and13,141 versus19,762bytes. Retain that existing capability; it is not yet proof
of its newly integrated CIX path.

The research portfolio is faster and uses less memory in those small measured
runs, so its size loss must not erase the potential speed/size trade-off. However,
it remains runtime-pinned Python research without a portable native format,
malformed-input qualification or current-CIX effort comparison. Retain as
research-only, not a new release route. Independent bzip2 savings are only20bytes
against original and15bytes against PAQ on one unique asset; also research-only.
The earlier fixed ZIP transfer expanded86.894% and remains excluded. Complete
costs, source-inaccessible exact decode and terminal decisions are recorded at
../2026-10-04-native-rc/codec-reconstruction-closure-v1/REPORT.txt.
